      What to notice

      Four habits this teaches in ten minutes.

      Decide the exit first

      The stop goes where your idea is proven wrong. The size then follows from it, so every loss is small and planned.

      Reward must outweigh risk

      At 2:1 you break even winning about a third of your trades. Move the target and watch the reward-to-risk number.

      Match the market

      Try the same plan in an uptrend and in a range. A strategy that shines in one can bleed in the other.

      Stops are not guarantees

      In the crash market, a gap can carry price straight through your stop and you are filled at the open. That is real risk.

      The real thing
