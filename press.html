Descriptions

Ready to quote

Short (43 words)

Pulse is an iPhone app that teaches people to trade and invest. It rates stocks, crypto and currencies from strong sell to strong buy, explains every rating in plain English, and lets users practise with a paper account before they risk real money.

Long (107 words)

Pulse combines three things most trading apps keep apart: education, practice and analysis. Its Academy runs from what a share is to options pricing and quantitative trading systems, across 74 courses and 686 lessons with exams and certificates. Its paper trading account uses live prices with spreads, fees, margin and short selling, and four challenges modelled on funded-trader evaluations. Signals score seven factors and come with a plan and the reasons behind it, and an AI analyst answers questions using live market data. Pulse is not a broker, never places real trades and earns nothing from trading activity. It is free for now and shows no ads.
