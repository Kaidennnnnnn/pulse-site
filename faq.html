About Pulse

What is Pulse?

Pulse is an iPhone app for learning to trade and invest. It combines a full course, a paper trading account with real-world rules, and buy, hold and sell signals that explain their reasons. An AI analyst is built too, and is switched off in the early test version.

Who is it for?

Complete beginners who want to learn properly, and active traders who want a structured way to test ideas and fix habits. The placement test skips what you already know, and the specialist courses go as far as options pricing and testing your own system.

Is Pulse a broker? Can I trade real money?

No. Pulse never handles money and never places real trades. Every position is in a paper account with virtual money on real market prices.

If you decide to trade for real, you would open an account with a regulated broker. The Academy explains what to look for.

Which markets does Pulse cover?

Stocks and ETFs from major exchanges, major indices, popular cryptocurrencies, currency pairs and commodities such as gold and oil. The screener and heatmap focus on large US companies.

Are prices live?

While the app is open, prices refresh every few seconds for what you hold, have orders on or are looking at, every ten seconds for your watchlist and every couple of minutes for the wider market. Crypto streams in real time. Stocks, indices, currencies and commodities come from a public data source, so some exchanges are delayed by 15 minutes or more. Pulse shows whether each market is open, closed, or in pre-market or after-hours trading.

What do I need?

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

Which languages does Pulse support?

The app is available in English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Dutch, Russian, Turkish, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Arabic. Lessons are translated into your language the first time you open them, so some lesson text can take a moment to appear.

Signals and AI

How are signals calculated?

Pulse scores each asset on seven factors: trend, momentum, volume and flow, key levels, fundamentals, news and market context. It weighs them for your risk profile and time horizon to produce a rating from Strong sell to Strong buy, with a confidence score.

The plan's stop and target come from recent volatility and nearby support and resistance, and the position size comes from the share of your account you choose to risk on each trade.

Are the signals financial advice?

No. They are general, educational output from a scoring model. They do not know your finances, goals or circumstances, and they can be wrong. Pulse shows every reason behind a signal so you can judge it yourself and learn what to look for.

How accurate are the signals?

Nobody can predict markets reliably, and Pulse doesn't claim to. Instead, Pulse records every signal you see and checks it against what happened next, so the track record screen shows how well signals have actually worked for the assets you follow, misses included.

What can the AI analyst see?

The market data Pulse fetches for the question, such as quotes, charts, indicators, signals and headlines, and, when it's relevant, your paper positions and trade journal. It can't see other apps, your bank or who you are.

Can the AI place trades for me?

It can suggest a paper trade, an alert or a lesson as a button. Nothing happens until you tap it, and it can never trade real money.

Which AI does Pulse use?

The analyst, news briefs and coaching are built on Anthropic's Claude models.

Can the AI be wrong?

Yes. AI can misread data or state things with too much confidence. Pulse asks it to fetch live data before answering and to show its reasoning, but you should treat its answers as a second opinion to check.

Academy

I've never traded. Where do I start?

At Lesson 1.1, What is a stock? The first three levels are free and assume no knowledge at all. If you know some basics, take the placement test and Pulse starts you at the right level.

How long does the course take?

There are 5550 minutes of lessons in total, about 92 hours, in lessons of 4 to 11 minutes. A lesson or two a day finishes the core path in a few weeks.

Is there a teacher in the lessons?

Yes, if you want one. Lessons open as quick steps by default: one idea at a time, a question after every couple of ideas, and an answer that always explains itself. If you would rather be taught, switch the lesson style to Teacher in Settings: each lesson is then taken one idea at a time on a clean board, with the practice questions and the quiz and a reaction to each answer. In either style you can ask the teacher a question, and the answer comes from an AI (Claude) that has read the lesson you are on.

AI answers can be wrong, so check anything that matters. If you prefer to read, the third style is plain cards, from the lesson menu or in Settings. You can also turn on read-aloud: the teacher reads the board, picking out the sentence being read, using your iPhone's own voice and sending nothing anywhere.

Can I practise trading on a chart inside the lessons?

Yes. 44 lessons end with a Chart Trade. You answer a couple of questions about the chart, then choose to buy, sell short or stay out, drag your stop and target, and watch the next candles play out. You are marked on the quality of the plan, not on luck, and a good plan can still lose.

You can also play Chart Trade on its own from the Practice tab, with a new chart every time. The charts are simulated to show a pattern clearly and are not real market history, so they tell you nothing about future performance. For real past prices, use Chart Replay.

What are quick drills and market stories?

Quick drills are short sets of fresh questions: money sums such as profit and loss, position size and reward to risk, tap-in-order puzzles and trade plans with a flaw to find. They come in four levels, from beginner to expert, and Pulse brings up the kinds you miss most.

Market stories are short scenarios, such as a friend's hot tip or three losses in a row. You choose, see what follows and why, and finish with the takeaway. Nothing in either uses real money.

Does the teacher work offline?

The lesson, the exercises and the quiz work without a connection. Only the questions you ask the teacher need the AI, so those need an internet connection.

Are the certificates recognised qualifications?

No. A Pulse certificate shows you passed the exam for a course. It is not a professional or regulated qualification.

What's free in the Academy?

Levels 1 to 3 (16 lessons) with their exams, the placement test, the glossary, flashcards, quick drills, market stories, Chart Trade, Chart Replay, Guess the Move and case studies. Everything else is unlocked too: all 74 courses and 686 lessons are free for now.

Paper trading

How realistic is paper trading in Pulse?

Very, for a simulator. Orders fill at live prices with a bid-ask spread, slippage on market orders, commission and currency fees, and stocks only trade during market hours. Margin accounts pay interest, shorts pay a borrow fee, and margin calls happen.

What no simulator can copy is how it feels to have real money on the line, so start small when you move to real trading.

Can I reset my account?

Yes. In Pulse Settings, under Paper account, tap Start a new practice account. Your closed trades stay in your journal.

What are the challenges?

Four tests modelled on funded-trader evaluations. Each opens a separate account with a profit target, a daily loss limit, a maximum drawdown, a minimum number of trading days and a deadline. All four are open to everyone.

Does passing a challenge get me a funded account?

No. Pulse challenges are practice. Real funded-trader programmes are run by private firms that charge fees and set their own rules.

Can I practise short selling?

Yes. Shorting is switched on by default, and shorts pay a borrow fee as they would at a broker. Level 7 of the Academy teaches when shorting makes sense and how to manage the risks.

Privacy and data

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, inside Pulse's own storage. It's included in your device backups, and it isn't uploaded to our servers.

Do you show ads or sell data?

No. Pulse has no advertising and no tracking code, and we don't sell or share your data.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. That removes everything Pulse stored on your iPhone.

Cost

Is Pulse really free?

Yes, for now. Every feature is unlocked, there are no ads and there is no account to create. The AI features are switched off in the early test version.

Will there be paid plans later?

We may add optional paid plans in future. Nothing is ever charged unless you choose it, and we will explain any change in the app and on this site before it happens.
