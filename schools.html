Pulse / For schools

Teach trading the safe way.

For teachers, lecturers and finance clubs. Nothing here uses real money, and learners need no account. Here is what you can use in a classroom today.

What you can use today

Free beginner guides

16 plain-English guides with built-in questions, from "What is a stock?" to reading charts. Print them, project them or set them as homework.

Open the guides

A simulator everyone can open

Runs in any browser, with no sign-up and nothing stored. Good on school laptops and phones.

Open the simulator

Calculators and a Lab

Position size, risk and reward, margin, and a simulator that shows how fast a bad risk habit ends an account.

Open the Lab

The full Academy

74 courses and 686 lessons in the app, with exams, flashcards and a journal. Each learner uses their own copy.

See the course

Run a class challenge

Everyone opens the same link, trades the same market and compares results. No accounts and nothing to install.

Your link

Try it yourself

Same code, same market, every time. Ask learners to share their final result and the choices behind it, then discuss: who risked less, who used stops, who got lucky?

A four-lesson starter plan

  1. What markets are. Read What is a stock? and Bid, ask and the spread. Discuss who is on the other side of every trade.
  2. Orders and stops. Read Stop orders and stop-losses, then use the position size calculator on three imaginary trades.
  3. Practise. Run the class challenge on a crash market and compare who protected their account.
  4. Reflect. Take the quiz and talk through the wrong answers.

What is not available yet

There is no teacher dashboard, class roster or group billing, and Pulse does not collect learner data. If a school licence or classroom reporting would help you, tell us what you need.
