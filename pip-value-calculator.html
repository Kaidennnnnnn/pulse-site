What is one pip worth?

A pip is the smallest usual price move in a currency pair. Its value depends on the pair, your trade size and your account currency.

The formula

Pip value = pip size x units, converted into your account currency. For most pairs a pip is 0.0001, for pairs quoted in yen it is 0.01. Trading 10,000 units of EUR/USD, one pip is $1. If one dollar is worth £0.79, that pip is £0.79.

Why it matters

To size a forex trade you need the money value of the distance to your stop. Multiply the stop distance in pips by the pip value to get the money at risk, then adjust the size until it matches your risk rule.

Watch the leverage

Small pip values make forex feel safe. Leverage multiplies the position, so a few pips can cost a lot. Learn without it first.
