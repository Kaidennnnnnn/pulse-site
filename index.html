How it works

Learn it. Try it. Then decide.

  1. Learn the ground rules

    Take the 12-question placement test and Pulse starts you at the right level. Each lesson takes 4 to 11 minutes: short explanations, a real chart, exercises and a quiz, one idea at a time.

  2. Trade with paper money

    Trade real market prices with a virtual account. Set stops and targets, go long or short, and let Pulse grade your discipline as well as your profit.

  3. Decide with a second opinion

    Every signal comes with its reasons and a plan. Ask the AI analyst anything about a chart, a headline or your own trades, then make your own call.

Academy

A trading course that goes all the way.

Ten core levels take you from your first share to a written trading plan. 64 specialist courses, from beginner to expert, go deep into options, macro, crypto, valuation, property, retirement, tax, risk, regulation and building tested systems.

  • Your way. Lessons open as quick steps, one idea at a time, with an answer that always explains itself. Want a teacher with a voice that picks out each sentence? Or just plain cards? Switch in one tap.
  • Hands-on, not just reading. Lessons end with calculations and real-world scenarios you work through, then a quiz that explains every answer. 44 lessons also put you on a chart: read it, plan the trade and watch it play out.
  • Practice that feels like a game. Fresh number drills, trade-plan puzzles and short market stories turn the ideas into habits, with a daily streak and three small quests to keep you coming back. Nothing uses real money, and a wrong answer always explains itself.

Practice

Trade like it's real. Lose nothing.

Your paper account trades live prices with real-world rules: spreads and fees, market and limit orders, stops and trailing stops, short selling, margin with interest, and margin calls if you overreach.

  • Every trade scored in R, the risk you planned, so you learn to judge decisions as well as results.
  • Automatic trade management: take half at +1R and move your stop to breakeven without lifting a finger.
  • A journal that talks back. Pulse spots cutting winners early, revenge trades within an hour of a loss, stops set too tight and overtrading days.

Pulse earns nothing when you trade

There are no commissions, no payment for order flow and no broker partners. Pulse has no ads and takes no cut of anything you do, so it has no reason to make you trade more.

Pulse is not a broker and never places real trades. Every position is paper money, so mistakes cost you nothing but a lesson.

Every signal shows its working

Questions

Before you ask.

Is Pulse a broker? Can I trade real money?

No. Pulse is a learning and practice app. Every trade happens in a paper account with virtual money, on real market prices. When you feel ready to trade for real, you would do that with a regulated broker of your choice.

Are the buy and sell signals financial advice?

No. Signals come from a transparent scoring model that weighs trend, momentum, volume, key levels, fundamentals, news and market context. They are general and educational, they can be wrong, and they do not know your circumstances. Pulse shows the reasons so you can judge them yourself.

I've never traded. Is it too advanced for me?

Pulse is built for complete beginners. The first lesson explains what a share is, and the first three levels are free. If you already know the basics, the placement test starts you further along.

Do the lessons have a teacher?

If you want one. Lessons open as quick steps by default: one idea at a time, a question after every couple of ideas, and an answer that always explains itself. Switch the lesson style to Teacher in Settings and each lesson is taken one idea at a time on a clean board, with read-aloud that picks out the sentence being read. In either style you can ask the teacher a question, and the answer comes from an AI (Claude) that has read the lesson you are on. AI answers can be wrong, so check anything that matters. If you prefer to read, the third style is plain cards, from the lesson menu or in Settings.

Is Pulse free?

Yes, for now. Everything is unlocked: all 74 courses and 686 lessons, the six research labs, every challenge, and unlimited alerts and practice accounts. There are no ads and no account to create.
