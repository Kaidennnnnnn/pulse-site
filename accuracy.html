How accurate is paper trading in Pulse?

Close enough to teach good habits, and honest about where it is not a broker statement. Here is exactly what is simulated, and what is not.

What the simulator does

AreaHow it works
FillsYou buy at the ask and sell at the bid. The half-spread is 0.02% for stocks, ETFs and indices, 0.05% for crypto, 0.005% for currencies and 0.03% for commodities.
SlippageMarket orders also pay 2% of the day's range, plus up to 0.2% when the order is large against daily volume. Limit orders do not slip.
Stops and targetsChecked on every price update (about every 15 seconds while the app is open). A stop is a market order, so a gap through it fills at the next price, which is worse.
While the app is closedWhen you reopen it, Pulse replays the price bars since it last looked (5 minute bars for short gaps, 15 minute then hourly for longer ones) so stops, targets and orders fill at the price and time they would have. If one bar reaches both stop and target, the stop is assumed first.
Shorting and marginShort sales pay a borrow fee. Margin accounts pay interest on borrowed cash. If equity falls below 25% of open exposure, the biggest loser is closed in a margin call.
Corporate actionsStock splits rescale your position, stop and target so nothing is won or lost. Dividends are paid to long positions and charged to shorts.
Costs and currenciesCommission on each trade and a currency fee on foreign stocks, both set by you. Prices in pence and in dollars are converted into your account currency.
Discipline rulesDaily loss limits lock new trades, challenge accounts enforce profit targets and drawdowns, and every trade can carry a stop, target and your reason for entering.

What it does not do

  • Live prices. Quotes come from public market-data feeds. Some are delayed by 15 minutes or more, and Pulse labels them.
  • The real order book. Spreads are typical, not quoted live. There is no queue position, so a limit order is filled when the last price reaches it, which can be a little generous.
  • Exchange-rate gains. If you hold a foreign stock, profit or loss from the currency moving is not tracked precisely.
  • Every corporate action. Mergers, spin-offs and cash paid for fractional shares after a reverse split are not handled.
  • Your emotions. Fake money feels different. The journal exists partly to notice that gap.

Treat results as a close simulation, not a broker statement. Pulse never places real trades and does not give financial advice.
