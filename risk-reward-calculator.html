The idea

Reward to risk (R:R) is the distance to your target divided by the distance to your stop. A £15 target and a £5 stop is 3:1.

Break-even win rate

Break-even = 1 / (1 + R:R). At 2:1 you break even winning only a third of your trades. At 1:1 you need to win more than half, before costs. At 3:1, a quarter is enough.

Expectancy

Expectancy = win rate x reward - loss rate x risk, measured in R. A positive number means the plan makes money over many trades. A negative one means no position sizing can save it.

Be honest about targets

A 5:1 target in the middle of nowhere is fantasy. Put targets at real price levels where sellers have appeared before.
