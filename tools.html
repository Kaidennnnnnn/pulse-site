Reward and edge

What win rate does my strategy need?

The bigger your average win compared with your average loss, the less often you need to be right.

Break-even win rate = 1 ÷ (1 + reward to risk). Expectancy = win rate × reward − loss rate × 1, in R.

Recovery

How much do I need to make it back?

Losses and gains aren't symmetrical. Falling 50% needs a 100% gain just to get back to where you started.

%
%

Gain needed = 1 ÷ (1 − loss) − 1. Years = log(1 + gain needed) ÷ log(1 + yearly return).
