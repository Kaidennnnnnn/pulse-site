Interactive tools that show you the maths behind trading. Drag a slider, see the consequence. The same ideas are taught in the Academy.

Survival maths

How likely is a blow-up?

Four hundred imaginary traders follow the same system with the same luck. Change the win rate, the size of winners and the risk per trade, and watch how many survive.

Losers always lose 1R. R is the amount you risk on one trade, so 2R means winning twice what you risked.

See any options strategy at a glance.
