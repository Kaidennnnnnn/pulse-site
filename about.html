Pulse / About

Teach the risk first.

Pulse exists because it has become very easy to start trading and no easier to learn how. We are building the course we think every beginner should take before they risk real money.

Why we built it

A phone can open a trading account in minutes.

Learning what a spread is, how leverage multiplies a loss or why a stop-loss matters usually comes later, and often the expensive way. Pulse turns that order round: learn the idea, practise it with virtual money, and only then decide for yourself.

Learn

74 courses and 686 short lessons, from the first share to a written trading plan.

See the syllabus

Practise

A paper account with realistic costs and rules, and help at the moment a decision is made.

What we will not do

Five rules we hold ourselves to.

  • No real money. Pulse is not a broker, never places a real trade and has no link that opens a brokerage account.
  • No promises of profit. Most active retail traders lose money. Our lessons say so, and so does our marketing.
  • No advice. Pulse teaches and explains. It does not tell anyone what to buy or sell with their own money.
  • No trade in personal data. Lessons, practice trades and the journal stay on the learner's own phone. There are no adverts.
  • No hiding the limits. Where something is simulated, delayed, unfinished or untested, we say so on the page.
Where we are
