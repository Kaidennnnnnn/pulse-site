What this means-

The formulas

Margin = position value / leverage. A £10,000 position at 5:1 needs £2,000 of your own money. The price move that uses up that margin is 100 / leverage percent, here 20%.

Why leverage hurts

A 10% fall on a £10,000 position loses £1,000. On a £3,000 account that is a third of everything, even though the market moved only 10%.

A safer habit

Size by risk, not by what leverage allows. Most brokers must publish the share of retail leveraged accounts that lose money, and it is usually the majority.
