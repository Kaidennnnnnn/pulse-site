Place your first paper trade

  1. Open any asset from Markets.
  2. Tap Paper trade. Pulse suggests a size from your risk rule.
  3. Check the stop-loss and target, or set your own.
  4. Tap Place order. Your trade appears in Portfolio, and on your lock screen as a Live Activity.

Add a Pulse widget

  1. Touch and hold an empty part of your home screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Pulse.
  3. Pick a widget and size, then tap Add Widget.
  4. To change the asset on a single-asset widget, touch and hold it and choose Edit Widget.

Turn on alerts

  1. When Pulse asks to send notifications, tap Allow. If you said no before, open the Settings app, then Notifications, then Pulse.
  2. On any asset page, scroll to Alerts and tap Add.
  3. Switch the morning brief and breaking-news alerts on or off in Pulse Settings.

An alert didn't arrive

Pulse checks alerts while the app is open and during background refreshes, which iOS schedules, so an alert can arrive a few minutes late.

Pulse sends no more than six market alerts a day and none between 22:00 and 07:30. Focus modes and Low Power Mode can also hold notifications back.

Start a challenge

  1. Go to Portfolio and tap Challenges.
  2. Read the rules for each challenge and tap Start challenge.
  3. A new account opens with the challenge's balance. Your progress shows at the top of Portfolio.

Reset your paper account

  1. Open Pulse Settings and scroll to Paper account.
  2. Tap Start a new practice account and choose a starting balance.
  3. Your closed trades and journal stay as they are.

Lock Pulse with Face ID

Open Pulse Settings and switch on Lock with Face ID. Pulse then asks for Face ID or your passcode whenever it opens.

Delete your data

Your data lives on your iPhone, so deleting the app deletes it.

System requirements

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later. An internet connection is needed for prices, news and AI features. Lessons work offline, apart from their live charts.
