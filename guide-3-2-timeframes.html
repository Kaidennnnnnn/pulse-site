Pulse / Learn / Level 3

Timeframes

The same stock can be in an uptrend, a downtrend and a range at the same time, depending on the chart you open.

Level 3: Reading charts · 4 min read · Chart reading

Different stories

A 5-minute chart shows today's noise; a daily chart shows weeks of trend; a weekly chart shows the big picture.

Top-down analysis

Start on the higher timeframe for direction, then use a lower one for timing. Trade in the direction of the bigger trend.

Match your horizon

Swing traders (days to weeks) use daily charts. Intraday traders use hourly and below. Pulse's signal shows all three views.

Remember this

  • Higher timeframes show direction; lower ones help with timing.
  • Trade in the direction of the bigger trend.
  • Match your chart timeframe to how long you plan to hold.
