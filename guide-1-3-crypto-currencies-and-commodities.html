Crypto, currencies and commodities

Bitcoin trades on Christmas Day. Gold reacts to interest rates. The pound moves on Bank of England decisions. Different markets, different rules.

Level 1: Market foundations · 5 min read · Fundamentals

Crypto

Cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin trade 24/7 on many exchanges. They have no earnings, so prices run on adoption, liquidity and sentiment, and swings of 5% a day are normal.

Currencies (forex)

FX trades in pairs: EUR/USD at 1.08 means one euro buys 1.08 dollars. It's the world's most traded market and moves on interest rates and economic data.

Commodities

Gold, oil and silver trade through futures contracts. Gold often rises when real interest rates fall or fear rises; oil follows supply, demand and geopolitics.

Volatility differs

A 2% day is huge for EUR/USD, normal for a stock, and quiet for Bitcoin. Always size trades to the asset's normal movement.

Remember this

  • Crypto trades 24/7 and runs on adoption, liquidity and sentiment, not earnings.
  • Forex trades in pairs and moves on interest rates and economic data.
  • Size every trade to the asset's normal movement, because volatility differs hugely.

Test yourself

1. EUR/USD at 1.10 means...

The first currency is the base: one euro costs 1.10 dollars.

2. Which market trades every day of the year?

Crypto exchanges never close.

3. Why must position size depend on the asset?

The same money in Bitcoin carries far more daily movement than in a currency pair.
