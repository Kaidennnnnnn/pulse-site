Privacy policy

How Pulse handles your information, in plain English.

Last updated 19 November 2026

Early test version. This page describes how the test version of Pulse works today. It will be reviewed again before the public release on the App Store.

The short version

  • Your watchlist, paper trades, journal, alerts and lesson progress are stored on your iPhone. We don't receive them.
  • Pulse has no account sign-up, no advertising and no tracking code. We never sell your data.
  • The AI features are switched off in the early test version, so nothing is sent to an AI provider unless you add your own API key in Settings. When they are switched on, the text of your request and the information needed to answer it will be sent to our AI provider to produce a reply, without your name or contact details.
  • Pulse is free for now and takes no payments. If paid plans are added, Apple will handle them and we will never see your card details.
  • You can delete everything Pulse stores by deleting the app.

Who we are

Pulse is published by Tristan Montialoux ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us about privacy at privacy@pulselearning.app.

This policy covers the Pulse iPhone app and this website.

Information kept on your device

Pulse keeps the following in its own storage on your iPhone so the app can work: your watchlist and settings, paper trading accounts, orders, positions and closed trades, your trade journal (including notes, setup tags and emotions you choose to record), price alerts, lesson progress, quiz results, XP and certificates.

This information is not sent to us. It is included in your iPhone backups according to your own backup settings, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Information used to provide features

Market data and news

To show prices, charts and news, the app requests data for the symbols you view from market data providers. These requests contain the symbols and the technical details every internet request carries, such as your IP address. We don't add any personal information to them.

AI features

The AI features (the analyst, the news briefs and the AI teacher) are switched off in the early test version of Pulse, so the app sends nothing to an AI provider on our behalf. The one exception is if you enter your own Claude API key in Settings: your requests then go straight from your iPhone to Anthropic under your own account, and we do not see them.

When they are switched on, a question you ask the AI analyst, or another AI feature such as a news brief or a coach report, will send the text of your request and the information needed to answer it to our AI provider, Anthropic, PBC, which will process it on our behalf and return a reply. Depending on your question, this can include market data and details from your paper trades or journal. We will not include your name, email address or other contact details. We will update this policy before the AI features are switched on.

Notifications

Price alerts, the morning brief and news alerts are created and delivered by the app on your iPhone. We don't run a server that tracks your alerts.

Purchases

Pulse is free for now and takes no payments. If paid plans are added, Apple will process the payment and tell the app whether you have an active subscription. We won't receive your card details.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us crash reports and usage statistics that don't identify you.

While Pulse is in testing, it is installed through Apple's TestFlight. If you send feedback or a screenshot from TestFlight, or choose to share details after a crash, Apple passes that information to us so that we can fix the problem.

When you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and what you tell us to answer you. We keep support conversations for up to two years after they end, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

This website

This website does not use cookies, advertising or analytics trackers, and its fonts are served from our own site. Our hosting provider, GitHub, keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, for security and to keep the site running.

If we offer a sign-up for launch news and you join it, we use your email address only to send you news about Pulse. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing deletes your address from the list.

Legal bases

Under UK and EU data protection law, we rely on:

  • Contract: to provide the features you ask for, including AI replies.
  • Legitimate interests: to answer support requests, keep our services secure and fix problems.
  • Consent: for launch-news emails and notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Who we share data with

We use a small number of service providers to run Pulse. They handle personal data only as far as their service needs it:

ProviderPurpose
Anthropic, PBCProducing replies for AI features (switched off in the early test version)
GitHub, Inc.Hosting this website

We may also disclose information if the law requires it. We never sell personal data or share it for advertising.

International transfers

Some providers process data outside the UK and the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where that happens, we rely on recognised safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to how we use it, to receive it in a portable form, and to withdraw consent. Because most of your Pulse data never leaves your iPhone, you can see and delete it yourself in the app.

To make a request, write to privacy@pulselearning.app. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK (ico.org.uk) or to your local data protection authority.

Children

Pulse is designed for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has contacted us, write to us and we will delete the information.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS. On your iPhone, Pulse's data is protected by iOS, and you can add a Face ID or passcode lock in Pulse Settings.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change matters, we will also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
