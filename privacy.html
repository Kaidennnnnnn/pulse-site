Privacy policy

How Pulse handles your information, in plain English.

Last updated 23 November 2026

The short version

  • Your watchlist, paper trades, journal, alerts and lesson progress are stored on your iPhone.
  • Pulse has no account sign-up and no advertising. Nothing follows you across other apps or websites, and we never sell your data.
  • Usage data is your choice. If you agree, we receive how you use the app (for example which lessons you open and when) under a random ID, without your name or contact details. You can switch it off and delete it in Settings at any time.
  • The AI features are switched off in the early test version, so nothing is sent to an AI provider unless you add your own API key in Settings. When they are switched on, the text of your request and the information needed to answer it will be sent to our AI provider to produce a reply, without your name or contact details.
  • Pulse is free for now and takes no payments. If paid plans are added, Apple will handle them and we will never see your card details.
  • What does leave your iPhone: requests for prices and news for the symbols you look at, and anything you choose to send or share yourself, such as a support email or a progress report.
  • You can delete everything Pulse stores by deleting the app.

Who we are

Pulse is published by Tristan Montialoux ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us about privacy at privacy@pulselearning.app.

This policy covers the Pulse iPhone app and this website.

Information kept on your device

Pulse keeps the following in its own storage on your iPhone so the app can work: your watchlist and settings, paper trading accounts, orders, positions and closed trades, your trade journal (including notes, setup tags and emotions you choose to record), price alerts, lesson progress, quiz results, XP, streaks and certificates, and a study group code if you enter one. If you add your own API keys in Settings, they are kept in the iOS Keychain.

This information is not sent to us, except as described below under "Usage data" and "Competitions". It is included in your iPhone backups according to your own backup settings, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Information used to provide features

Market data and news

To show prices, charts and news, the app requests data for the symbols you view straight from market data providers: Yahoo Finance for prices and charts, Coinbase for live crypto prices, and Finnhub for news, company data and live share prices where a Finnhub key is set. These requests go from your iPhone to the provider. They contain the symbols and the technical details every internet request carries, such as your IP address. We don't add your name or any other personal information to them, and we don't see them. Each provider handles them under its own privacy policy.

Usage data (only if you agree)

After the welcome screens, Pulse asks whether you want to share usage data. Nothing is sent unless you say yes, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings, under Privacy.

If you agree, the app sends our server a record of how you use it: the screens and lessons you open and how long you spend on them, lessons started, finished or left, your quiz and exercise answers, XP, streak and course progress, your practice trades (the symbol, the direction and how the trade ended, never an amount of money), that you asked the AI a question (never the words), your settings, and which reminders you received, opened or followed with a lesson. Each record carries the time, your time zone, the app version, your iPhone model and iOS version, your language and region, and a random ID created by the app. Our server adds the country and region your connection comes from and does not keep your IP address.

We do not receive your name, email address, contacts, photos, exact location, advertising identifier, anything you type to the AI or in your journal, or any real holdings or balances.

We use this to understand which lessons work and where people get stuck, to fix problems, and to improve the timing and wording of reminders. We build a summary for each random ID, for example how often and at what times of day the app is used. We do not use it for advertising and we do not share it with anyone except the provider that runs our server.

In Settings, under Privacy, you can switch sharing off, download a copy of what we hold for your iPhone, or delete it. Switching sharing off also deletes it from our server.

Competitions

If you create or join a paper trading competition, our server keeps the display name you chose, the app's random ID and the results your app reports (return, largest fall and number of trades), so it can show the table to the other members. A private competition is visible to its members only. Competitions are deleted 90 days after they end.

Translation and read-aloud

Lessons in other languages are translated by Apple's translation feature on your iPhone, and lessons are read aloud by the iPhone's own voices. Neither sends your lessons or answers to us. The first time you use a language, iOS may download that language from Apple.

Study groups and sharing

A study group code is a label kept on your iPhone. It is not an account and it is not sent to us. A progress report, a trade card or a certificate leaves your iPhone only when you tap Share and choose where to send it. What happens to it then depends on the app and the person you send it to.

AI features

The AI features (the analyst, the news briefs and the AI teacher) are switched off in the early test version of Pulse, so the app sends nothing to an AI provider on our behalf. The one exception is if you enter your own Claude API key in Settings: your requests then go straight from your iPhone to Anthropic under your own account, and we do not see them.

When they are switched on, a question you ask the AI analyst, or another AI feature such as a news brief or a coach report, will send the text of your request and the information needed to answer it to our AI provider, Anthropic, PBC, which will process it on our behalf and return a reply. Depending on your question, this can include market data and details from your paper trades or journal. We will not include your name, email address or other contact details. We will update this policy before the AI features are switched on.

Notifications

Price alerts, the morning brief, news alerts and learning reminders are created and delivered by the app on your iPhone. We don't run a server that tracks your alerts. To time learning reminders, the app works out on your iPhone when you usually study; this stays on your iPhone unless you have agreed to share usage data.

Purchases

Pulse is free for now and takes no payments. If paid plans are added, Apple will process the payment and tell the app whether you have an active subscription. We won't receive your card details.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us crash reports and usage statistics that don't identify you.

While Pulse is in testing, it is installed through Apple's TestFlight. If you send feedback or a screenshot from TestFlight, or choose to share details after a crash, Apple passes that information to us so that we can fix the problem.

When you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and what you tell us to answer you. We keep support conversations for up to two years after they end, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

This website

This website does not use cookies, advertising or analytics trackers, and its fonts are served from our own site. Our hosting provider, GitHub, keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, for security and to keep the site running.

If we offer a sign-up for launch news and you join it, we use your email address only to send you news about Pulse. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing deletes your address from the list.

Legal bases

Under UK and EU data protection law, we rely on:

  • Contract: to provide the features you ask for, including AI replies.
  • Legitimate interests: to answer support requests, keep our services secure and fix problems.
  • Consent: for sharing usage data, launch-news emails and notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Who we share data with

We use a small number of service providers to run Pulse. They handle personal data only as far as their service needs it:

We may also disclose information if the law requires it. We never sell personal data or share it for advertising.

International transfers

Some providers process data outside the UK and the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where that happens, we rely on recognised safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep things

  • On your iPhone: until you delete it in the app or delete the app.
  • Usage data: up to 400 days, or until you switch sharing off or delete it in Settings.
  • Competitions: until 90 days after the competition ends, or until you leave it.
  • Support emails: up to two years after the conversation ends, or sooner if you ask.
  • Website server logs: kept by GitHub under its own retention rules.
  • TestFlight feedback and crash reports: for as long as we need them to fix the problem, and no longer than two years.

Automated decisions and profiles

The signals, grades and scores in Pulse are calculated on your iPhone from market data and your own practice. The app also works out on your iPhone when you usually study and which reminder messages you respond to, so that reminders arrive at a useful time and stop when they are not helping.

If you share usage data, we keep a summary of how each random ID uses Pulse. We use it to improve the app, not to make decisions about you: nothing in Pulse has a legal or similarly significant effect on you, and no offer or price depends on it.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to how we use it, to receive it in a portable form, and to withdraw consent. Because most of your Pulse data never leaves your iPhone, you can see and delete it yourself in the app. Usage data can be switched off, downloaded and deleted in Settings, under Privacy; the reference shown there helps us find it if you write to us.

To make a request, write to privacy@pulselearning.app. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK (ico.org.uk) or to your local data protection authority.

Children

Pulse is designed for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has contacted us, write to us and we will delete the information.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS. On your iPhone, Pulse's data is protected by iOS, and you can add a Face ID or passcode lock in Pulse Settings. Pulse does not use iCloud, so apart from what this policy describes, your data is not copied to our systems or to your other devices by the app.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change matters, we will also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
