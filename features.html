Drills and stories

Practice that feels like a game.

Quick, fresh questions and short scenarios that turn the ideas into habits. Nothing here uses real money, and a wrong answer always explains itself.

Number drills
Fresh sums every time: profit and loss, percentage change, money at risk, where a stop goes, shorting, leverage, position size, reward to risk, recovering from a loss, expectancy, sizing from account risk, what an edge is worth and the break-even win rate
Four levels
Each drill belongs to beginner, intermediate, advanced or expert, and the set you pick leans on that level
Learns what you miss
Pulse notes which kinds of drill you get wrong at the first try and brings them up more often, while keeping the easier ones in the mix
In the lessons too
Lessons on stops, shorting, leverage, position sizing and reward to risk carry their own drills, right after the idea they practise
Order and flaws
Tap the steps of a trade in the right order, or find the flawed line in a trade plan, from a first trade to a backtest that was tuned too far
Market stories
Short branching scenarios, such as a friend's hot tip or three losses in a row. You choose, see what follows and why, and finish with the takeaway and a link to the lesson
Fix your mistakes
A quiz question you miss in a lesson is remembered and comes back in a short session until you get it right
Quests and streaks
Three small quests a day with a bonus for finishing them, a streak that a weekly freeze can protect, and a lesson goal you set yourself

Lesson styles

Three ways to take a lesson.

Lessons open as quick, game-like steps. If you would rather be taught, or just read, you can change the style in Settings or from the lesson menu at any time.

Quick lessons
The default: one idea at a time, a question after every couple of ideas, and an answer that always explains itself. Your guide, Aurel, greets you, cheers a right answer and is kind about a wrong one
Play with it
In lessons on position size, losing streaks, recovering from a loss, compounding, fees and leverage you drag sliders and watch the numbers move, with a goal to reach. A button shows the answer if you want it
Stuck? Peek
Every question has a button that shows the lesson card you need, so you can look it up without leaving the question. A peek is fine: that question just does not count towards your streak
Teacher style
Prefer to be taught? Each idea is taken in turn on a clean board, with the worked example and the chart. Turn on read-aloud and the sentence being read is picked out as it goes, using your iPhone's own voice and sending nothing anywhere
Ask anything
Ask your teacher a question at any point, or start from a quick one: explain it more simply, give me an example, quiz me. Answers come from an AI that has read the lesson you are on
Your way
Prefer to read? Switch to plain cards from the lesson menu or in Settings
Works offline
The lesson, the exercises and the quiz need no connection. Only questions to the teacher use the AI
Your language
The teacher speaks the app's language, and lessons are translated into it as you open them
Care
The teacher teaches and does not advise. AI answers can be wrong, so check anything that matters

Chart Trade

Analyse the chart. Then trade it.

Read a chart, plan a trade on it and watch it play out. It is part of 44 lessons, and it is a practice screen of its own with a new chart every time.

Read it first
Each chart starts with questions: what is the trend, which line is the level that matters. You answer before you can trade, and every answer is explained
Plan the trade
Choose to buy, sell short or stay out, then drag the stop and the target on the chart. The ticket shows the reward to risk and the size that risks 100 dollars
Watch it play out
The next candles appear one by one until your stop or your target is hit. The result is shown in R, the amount you risked
Marked on process
A good plan has a stop beyond the level that proves the idea wrong and a target that pays for the risk. Luck does not earn the mark, and sometimes the right answer is to stay out
Eight patterns, both ways
Pullbacks, ranges, breakout retests, fake breakouts, double tops and bottoms, flags, choppy markets and stretched moves, each rising or falling
Works offline
Each chart is drawn on your phone from a seed, so it needs no connection and looks the same for everyone
Honest about what it is
The charts are simulated to show one pattern clearly. They are not real market history, so use Chart Replay for real past prices. Practice only

Paper trading

A practice account that behaves like a real one.

Trade live prices with virtual money and real-world rules, then let Pulse show you what your habits are costing you.

Orders
Market, limit, stop, stop-limit and trailing stop, valid for the day or until cancelled
Directions
Go long or short on stocks, ETFs, crypto, currencies and commodities
Accounts
Cash accounts, plus 2× margin and 4× day-trading margin, and as many practice accounts as you like
Real costs
Bid-ask spread, slippage on market orders, commission, a 0.15% currency fee, 7% yearly margin interest and a 3% short borrow fee
Risk rules
A daily loss limit, a cap on open positions, and a margin call that closes your biggest loser if equity falls below 25% of your positions
Trade management
Stops and targets, trailing stops, closing 25% or 50%, adding to a position, moving the stop to breakeven, and taking half at +1R
Help at the ticket
As you set up a trade, Pulse checks it against your own rules. It points out a missing stop or one on the wrong side, a trade bigger than your risk limit, a large position, or a trade placed soon after a loss. Most come with a one-tap fix and a Why? that opens the lesson
Journal
Tag the setup and your emotions, note what you learned and whether you followed your plan
Your habits
After six closed trades the Journal compares your first trades with your latest: how often you used a stop, how much you risked, how often you followed your plan and how many trades started on impulse. It is about habits, not profit, because a few practice trades cannot show skill
Analytics
Win rate, expectancy, profit factor, Sharpe, drawdown and an R histogram, plus results by setup, emotion, direction, holding time, hour, weekday and asset
Excursion
The best and worst point of every trade, to show whether your stops are too tight or your exits too early

Research labs

Six labs for the questions behind a trade.

Each lab explains its results in plain English, with an Ask AI button for anything unclear.

Strategy Lab

Six classic strategies backtested on up to ten years of data, with fees, compared with buy and hold: moving-average cross, RSI mean reversion, Donchian breakout, MACD trend, Bollinger bounce and 12-month momentum.

Portfolio Backtester

Monthly investing and rebalancing for any mix of assets, with presets for the S&P 500, 60/40, three-fund, All Weather, Golden Butterfly, tech growth and a 10% Bitcoin tilt.

Setup Scanner

Nine setups found and ranked each day: 52-week high breakout, golden cross, pullback in an uptrend, oversold bounce, volatility squeeze, volume surge, MACD turn, death cross and 52-week low breakdown.

Options Lab

Eleven strategies priced with Black-Scholes and live volatility: long calls and puts, covered calls, protective and cash-secured puts, bull and bear spreads, straddles, strangles, iron condors and butterflies.

Risk Lab

Volatility, worst-day loss and correlations across your positions, crash tests that apply falls the size of 2008 and 2020 to your positions, and a simulator that runs your account forward along 1,000 random paths.

Compare

Up to four assets on one chart, with return, volatility, worst fall and Sharpe ratio side by side.
